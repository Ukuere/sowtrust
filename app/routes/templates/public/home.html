How an order works

The payable amount is fixed only after the buyer sees and accepts the logistics quote.

1. Select produce

Choose a published product and confirm the required quantity and delivery destination.

2. Receive logistics quote

SowTrust Operations coordinates a quote from an eligible provider.

3. Accept and pay

The buyer accepts the final total before Paystack payment is initialized.

4. Confirm delivery

Delivery codes and server-confirmed payment states protect settlement.

Trust is visible

Registration, verification and listing publication are separate states. This keeps the market open while making risk clear.

Identity checks

Phone identity connects USSD and web activity. KYC status remains visible to operations and buyers.

Protected documents

KYC files are stored behind staff authorization and are never linked from public marketplace pages.

Locked quotes

A buyer-approved logistics quote cannot change silently after payment initialization.

Audited decisions

Moderation, payment and settlement changes create an operations audit trail.
