Frequently Asked Questions

Can an unverified farmer publish produce?

Yes. A valid listing may be published with a “verification pending” badge. Verification is shown separately and can later be approved, rejected or suspended.

When does the buyer pay?

Only after SowTrust Operations locks a logistics quote and the buyer explicitly accepts the final checkout total.

Can a logistics price change after payment starts?

No. The locked amount cannot change silently after payment initialization. A higher replacement quote requires a separate buyer approval process before a new payment is initialized.

I registered through USSD. Can I use the web portal?

Yes. Use the activation option for your role and verify the same phone number with a one-time code.

Is the displayed USSD code live?

Only a code explicitly labelled “Live SowTrust USSD” is public. Sandbox access is for approved testing and is labelled as such.
